# Manual accuracy rubric

Score each response independently against the case's `expectedPoints`. Score factual accuracy and relevance only; schema validity, source grounding, and Docker execution are reported separately by the harness.

| Score | Guidance |
|---|---|
| 0 | Incorrect, materially misleading, or misses the central point. |
| 1 | Partially correct, but incomplete or contains a meaningful inaccuracy. |
| 2 | Accurate and relevant; covers the essential expected points without material errors. |

Do not award points for fluency alone. When a case is still marked `PLACEHOLDER`, replace its expected points before assigning a score. Use the notes column to record a brief rationale or a source for disputed facts. Leave unscored cells blank; do not infer or invent scores from automated metrics.
